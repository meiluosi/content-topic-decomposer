# Reader stages

A topic reaches different people at different distances. The same idea fails if it is aimed at the wrong stage: a deep tutorial loses strangers, and a hot take does not help someone about to commit money or time. Tag every idea with one stage.

| Stage | State of mind | What they want | Types that lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Stranger** (doesn't know you) | Not looking for you. Scrolling, or has a vague pain. | A reason to stop: a sharp claim, a relatable moment, a surprising fact, one quick win. | Opinion, story, a very short tutorial (one trick, one myth) |
| **B. Just learned** (knows you or the topic exists) | Curious, wants orientation. | The map: what this is, how the parts fit, how to start. | Tutorial (foundations), beginner resource, series of explainers |
| **C. About to act** (comparing, deciding, afraid of a mistake) | Weighing cost and risk. | Evidence it works, honest limits, a decision rule, a pitfall list. | Case, decision-style tutorial, opinion on the choice, pitfall resource |
| **D. Already using** (doing it, stuck or optimizing) | Wants depth and shortcuts. | Troubleshooting, advanced technique, templates, peers. | Advanced tutorial, case with failures, resource (checklists/templates), opinion on edge debates |

## How to use the stages
- **Funnel check:** a healthy plan has ideas in all four stages. Most creators over-produce stage B (explainers) and under-produce A (nothing makes strangers stop) and C (no proof).
- **Next step:** every piece should hint at the next stage. A stage A story ends with a pointer to the stage B tutorial; a stage C case points to the stage D resource.
- **One stage per piece:** a piece aimed at everyone fits no one. If it spans stages, choose the primary one and let the rest be a bonus.

## Mapping to commercial topics
For topics tied to selling something, read the stages as: A = unaware of the problem, B = aware and researching, C = evaluating options (including you), D = existing customers. The content types stay the same.

## Mapping to non-commercial topics
If there is no product, read the stages as relationship to the topic or creator: A = never heard, B = first contact, C = about to try or follow along, D = practising it. The funnel logic is the same; "conversion" just means the next read, follow, share or try.
